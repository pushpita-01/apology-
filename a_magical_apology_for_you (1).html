Chapter 5 • Tinker Bell's Parchment Scroll

My Complete Apology Letter 💌

Dearest You,

I know I have made a huge mistake and hurt you. I know my mistake might be unforgivable, but still, I am begging you. Can't you forgive me? Can't you find it in your heart to forgive me? I don't even know whether I deserve your forgiveness. Maybe I deserve to be ignored by you. But even then, I am asking you, isn't there any way you could give me just one more chance? You may leave the university, but please don't leave my life. Please. I promise I will never complain about anything again. Whenever you are upset, I end up hurting even more than you do. I really can't take this anymore. Please forgive me. Just this once. I genuinely didn't understand that things would become this serious. It's my fault. I am such an idiot. I have accepted that I have no brain at all, that there is simply nothing inside my head. You told me not to message you, and now I don't even have the courage to text you anymore. You also said you wouldn't read any more long messages from me, and I accepted that. I won't send another long message, I promise. But I can at least send you the link, can't I? You never told me not to send the link. I know I was late in sending it, and I am sorry for that. You only matter in here. Please forgive me. Please meet me. I promise I won't do anything like this ever again. You said it depends on your mood. That's okay. Take your time, but please don't take too long. Honestly, I am not doing well anymore. You know what? Change your mood into killing mode and just kill me. That would be better, at least I wouldn't have to suffer like this, little by little, anymore. Every single moment, I keep thinking about the huge mistake I made and how badly I hurt you. Now I am suffering from the consequences of my own actions. When you don't talk to me, it keeps bothering me and hurting me constantly. Please forgive me. I will do whatever you say. Please, just forgive me this one last time. Human beings are a mixture of forgiveness, mistakes, and imperfections. I am no exception. Whatever punishment you give me for my mistake, I will accept it without complaining. But being left without a word like this is something I cannot bear. I could never handle it before, and I still cannot. If you can, please forgive me. I need you. I don't know how to go on without you. I want you in my life for as long as I live. You can scold me, hit me, even throw me into a river, but I would still want you. Because you are the one I love. You are my dearest person, a piece of my heart. If I could, I would place you above my head and keep you there. At the end of the day, all I want is you. No matter what happens, I would do anything to make you let go of your anger and resentment. If you told me to stay outside all day or all night just so you could forgive me, I would do it. You are that important to me. I admit that I react emotionally sometimes, and that is my mistake. You have tolerated so much from me, and I am genuinely grateful for that. If you can, please forgive me. I promise this will never happen again. I am sorry, I am so, so sorry. Take your time, but please don't take too long. I really cannot handle this anymore. Please. And honestly, being killed would be a better idea than being ignored like this. 🥴 This time, it was my mistake. I accept whatever comes my way. Do whatever you want with me, give me whatever punishment you think I deserve, but please, at least meet me once. That's all I am asking for.

Always yours, with all my heart ❤️
